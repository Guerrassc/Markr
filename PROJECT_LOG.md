## M1 - Problem and customer validation (due 2026-10-12; written 2026-10-18)

**Problem.** Every assignment comes with a brief mixing deliverables, deadlines, word limits, formatting rules and marking criteria. Students juggling several courses read it once and discover at the end that a requirement was missed. *[TODO: X of Y interviewees lost marks or redid work; X minutes per assignment]*

**Users and customers.** Users: university students. Payers: students (free tier plus small monthly fee); later universities or student services. Segment: University of Luxembourg 7,200+ students (uni.lu Facts & Figures); 35,615 Luxembourg students received state aid in 2024/25 (Paperjam). *[TODO: first target share]*

**Interviews.** *[TODO: 2+ student interviews, role, date, ~20-30 min, 5+ bullets each; one asks what they spend (time, marks, paid help)]*

**Alternatives.** Do nothing (reread the brief); own checklist or calendar app (deadlines only, no checking); general chatbot (inconsistent, nothing saved, work goes to a third party).

**Value proposition.** For university students juggling several assignments who lose marks to missed requirements, Markr is an assignment-checklist assistant that turns a brief or rubric into a list of every requirement and checks your draft against it in minutes, unlike rereading the brief by hand or pasting it into a general chatbot and hoping.

**Why AI.** Briefs have no standard format and many rules are only implied; extracting them and checking a draft needs language understanding that fixed rules cannot provide.
